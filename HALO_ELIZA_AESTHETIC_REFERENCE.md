# HALO — Eliza aesthetic reference

[Eliza Research](https://elizaresearch.ai/) was inspected in the browser on 13 September 2026 together with [Hermes](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/).

Eliza's opening uses saturated orange, very large bold white sans-serif copy, compact navigation and a pixel/particle figure. Its direct, spacious composition presents one strong idea before secondary information.

HALO adopts that clarity and saturation. Its own expression combines deep purple, orange emphasis, light condensed headings, engraved agent portraits and an inward-pointed ring. HALO's navigation, copy, economic diagram and agent screens are original implementations. It does not reproduce Eliza's particle figure or Hermes's blue palette and page structure.

The founder's [orange/violet palette](https://coolors.co/palette/ff6d00-ff7900-ff8500-ff9100-ff9e00-240046-3c096c-5a189a-7b2cbf-9d4edd) and HALO_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md govern the implementation. Prior green concepts are historical references.
